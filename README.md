# CSD-Certified-Scrum-Developer-Final-Project
CSD Certified Scrum Developer Final Project

> Money, debt and taxes, explained like a human would.

Nobody taught us how public debt works, why interest rates matter, or what that "buy now, pay later" button really costs. **[AppName]** is a mobile/web app that helps young people (15–25) understand public debt and manage their own money better, through short lessons, simulators and fun challenges.

## Why this exists

- Most young people don't understand public debt, even though it shapes the taxes they'll pay and the country they'll live in.
- Easy credit and "pay later" apps make it simple to get into debt early.
- Good financial education is hard to find: school barely covers it, and online content is either too technical or trying to sell you something.

We want to change that: neutral, simple and practical.

## Product description

[AppName] makes public debt and personal finance easy to understand for people aged 15–25. Users learn through 3-minute lessons, test their decisions in a monthly budget simulator, and stay motivated with quizzes, streaks and challenges. The content is neutral, jargon-free and based on real data, so users can see how debt, taxes and credit affect their lives and make better financial decisions.

## What you can do (planned)

- 📚 **Learn** – 3-minute lessons on public debt, deficit, taxes, saving and credit
- 🧮 **Simulate** – build a monthly budget and see how your choices play out
- 🎮 **Play** – quizzes, streaks and challenges to keep learning fun
- 📈 **Track progress** – see what you've learned and what's next

## Who it's for

Young people aged 15–25, from students to first-job workers, with no prior knowledge of finance.
